# Third-Party Risk Standard — Meridian Global Bank (example)

*Fictional example document.*

## Tiering

- **Tier 1 (critical):** outage or breach affects payments, core ledger, or customer funds. Examples: LedgerCore, SWIFT gateway provider.
- **Tier 2 (important):** affects customer data or a material business process.
- **Tier 3 (standard):** everything else.

## Requirements for Tier 1

- Independent assurance (SOC 2 Type II or equivalent) reviewed within the last 12 months — attestation alone is not sufficient evidence.
- A documented, tested exit plan with a maximum notice period of 12 months.
- Right to audit and breach notification within 24 hours.
- Annual concentration review by Vendor Risk with the CISO.

## Exceptions

Any deviation requires a named business owner, a compensating control, and an expiry date no longer than 12 months. Tier 1 exceptions are approved by the CISO and reported to the Board Risk Committee.
